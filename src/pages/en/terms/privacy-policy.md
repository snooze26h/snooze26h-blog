## Current status

This version runs locally. Analytics, comments, and user accounts are disabled, and the site does not intentionally collect personal information.

The Links page requests public avatars from the listed blogs. Once you follow an external link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

This notice will be updated before any analytics or comment feature is enabled on a public deployment.
